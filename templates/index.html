How HeartGuard Evaluates Your Risk

A seamless 3-step assessment designed to provide transparent, interpretable cardiovascular insights.

01

Enter Clinical Vitals

Provide routine health measurements including resting blood pressure, cholesterol, resting ECG findings, exercise tolerance, and fluoroscopy counts.

02

Machine Learning Inference

Features are scaled and one-hot encoded through an automated scikit-learn pipeline, evaluating patient metrics against hundreds of historical clinical outcomes.

03

Interpretable Risk Assessment

Receive an intuitive risk probability gauge, category badge (Low, Moderate, or High), and an explanation of the top clinical factors driving the outcome.

Key Capabilities

Built for Precision, Privacy & Clarity

Every design decision aligns with clinical interpretability and ease of use.

🛡️

Evidence-Based Model

Benchmarked across Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost, selecting the champion algorithm by Area Under the ROC Curve (96.65%).

📊

Interactive Gauge Chart

Visual semi-circular risk meter powered by Chart.js for rapid, at-a-glance comprehension of cardiovascular risk strata.

🔍

Top Influencing Factors

Transparent feature attribution exposes precisely which biomarkers elevated or attenuated the overall risk calculation.

⚡

Automated Input Validation

Physiological boundary checks ensure blood pressure, heart rates, and lab readings reflect real-world clinical bounds.

🔒

Strictly Private & Ephemeral

HeartGuard operates statelessly. No patient data or clinical inputs are persisted to disk or external databases.

🌐

Production Ready REST API

Seamlessly integrate risk scoring into external microservices or electronic health records via /api/predict.

Educational Project Notice

HeartGuard is developed strictly as an educational and demonstration project. It is not an FDA-cleared diagnostic device and should never replace professional medical judgment, laboratory testing, or in-person cardiology evaluation.
